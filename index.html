What a Production Engineer interview actually tests Meta PE ≈ SRE + software engineer. The "live technical" is typically a broad, conversational probe: they poke a topic (e.g. "what happens when you type a URL?", "a server has high load, what do you do?", "design a system to collect metrics from 1M hosts"), then keep asking "why?" and "what if that fails?" until you reach your limit. Breadth across systems, Linux internals, networking, and distributed system design matters more than any single framework. Expect to reason about failure modes, capacity, monitoring and debugging constantly. This site is built around that.

How to study with this site

Read the Basics pass first. Every section is tagged basics or deep dive. Do all basics across all pages before any deep dives — PE interviews reward breadth.
Play with every simulator until you can predict what will happen before you click. If you can predict it, you understand it.
Say answers out loud. Quizzes have hidden answers. Speak your answer before revealing — the interview is verbal.
Draw. After each case study, redraw the architecture from memory on paper in under 5 minutes.
Mark sections done (○ in the sidebar) to track coverage. Finish with the Interview Kit checklist.
